About Our Legacy

ঐতিহ্য, সংস্কৃতি ও মানবকল্যাণে আড়ংঘাটা শতদল ক্লাবের গৌরবময় পথচলা

52+ Years of Brotherhood

A Journey of Faith, Art & Selfless Service

Established in 1969 in the peaceful town of Aranghata within the Ranaghat subdivision of Nadia district, Aranghata Satadal Club (আড়ংঘাটা শতদল ক্লাব) was conceived with a heartfelt mission: to celebrate the divine spiritual grace of Mother Shyama, nurture Bengal's rich folk traditions, and serve our local community unconditionally.

What began five decades ago as an intimate neighborhood worship has blossomed into one of Nadia's premier festive destinations, welcoming over 1,00,000 visitors every autumn. Yet, amidst the glittering chandeliers and towering pandals, the club has never drifted from its foundational ethos: unconditional brotherhood and round-the-year public service.

Our Guiding Philosophy

"To unite hearts through sacred celebration, uplift every underprivileged neighbor through compassion, and preserve the artistic heritage of Bengal for future generations."
