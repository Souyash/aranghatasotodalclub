Location & Directions

অরংঘাটা শতদল ক্লাব প্রাঙ্গণে সহজে পৌঁছানোর যাবতীয় পথনির্দেশ ও গাইড

By Train (Sealdah - Gede Main Line)

রেলপথে আগমন

Board any local train towards Gede from Sealdah or Ranaghat Junction. De-board at Aranghata Railway Station.

The pandal is just 400m east of Platform 1 (5 minutes walk or Toto ride).

By Bus & Road (NH-34 / NH-12)

সড়কপথে আগমন

From Ranaghat Bus Stand (approx. 9 km), board frequent buses or auto-rickshaws heading towards Aranghata Bazar. Round-the-clock Toto and auto services operate during the puja days.

Designated Free Parking

নিরাপদ পার্কিং ব্যবস্থা

Designated parking for two-wheelers and four-wheelers is available at Aranghata High School Ground (200m from pandal entrance) under 24/7 CCTV surveillance and volunteer supervision.
